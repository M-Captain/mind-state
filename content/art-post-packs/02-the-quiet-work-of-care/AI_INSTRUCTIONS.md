# AI / Designer Instructions — The Quiet Work of Care

## Role of this pack
This is a MindState **Art post** pack: mainly visual. Use these images as collage raw material for the website Art section. Do **not** invent new historical captions; keep artwork titles and credits accurate.

## Rubric & palette
- **Rubric tag:** Care
- **Motif:** hands / vessel / pulse
- **Accent colour:** `#A67C52` (warm linen) — push recolour / overlays toward this hue; keep shadows soft.
- **Featured image:** `image_01_featured.jpg` (hero / card / OG). Supporting images: `image_02.jpg` … for carousel, collage layers, or detail crops.

## What to do
1. Open featured + 1–2 supporting images in **Figma** or **Canva**.
2. **Crop details** (eyes closed, hands, nerve lines, lettering, horizon) rather than always using the full plate.
3. **Recolour** lightly toward `#A67C52`: duotone, colour overlay at 20–40% opacity, or selective hue shift. Preserve paper grain when present.
4. **Collage** with understated paper textures, torn edges, margin doodles, or a large pull-quote from a MindState article (typography as art).
5. Keep composition calm: generous margins, one primary focal crop, optional thin museum-credit line in small type.
6. Export web assets (WebP/JPEG) at 2x; keep a layered source file for the design team.

## What NOT to do
- Do **not** alter historical caption facts or claim MindState owns the museum works.
- Do **not** remove credit; include a small credit on-page or in alt text (title + museum + Public Domain / CC0).
- Do **not** sensationalise medical or body imagery; keep a **therapeutic, non-graphic** tone.
- Do **not** use these for shock, gore, or stigmatising mental-illness tropes.
- Do **not** upscale with heavy AI “beautify” that invents faces or false details — gentle clean-up only.
- Do **not** exceed 5 images in the published post; one must remain featured.

## Suggested Canva / Figma workflow (short)
1. Create 1080×1350 (feed) and 1600×900 (hero) frames.
2. Place `image_01_featured.jpg` full-bleed; darken edges slightly.
3. Add motif crop from a supporting image at low opacity as texture.
4. Overlay accent colour `#`#A67C52 at ~25%.
5. Add post title in a quiet serif; optional pull-quote.
6. Footer credit: artwork title · museum · Public Domain / CC0.
7. Export + archive layered file beside this folder.

## Metadata files in this folder
- `post.json` — editorial fields for CMS
- `urls.json` — licenses, page URLs, download URLs
- `CREDITS.md` — human-readable credits
