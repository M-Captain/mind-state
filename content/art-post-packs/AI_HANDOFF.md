# MindState Art Posts — House Style Handoff

Sophia’s direction (summary): use **public-domain museum collages** as the house style. Prefer **Wellcome Collection** (history of medicine & psychology), then **Met Open Access**, Rijksmuseum, Smithsonian. Free, legally safe (PDM / CC0). Beginners can crop, recolour, and combine with typography and paper textures in Canva or Figma.

Also in the toolkit: **one motif + one accent colour per rubric**; optional team photos; typography / large pull quotes; scanned handmade elements (notes, margin doodles, crossed-out sentences, paper edges).

## Rules for every post
- Max **5** images; exactly **one** featured (`image_01_featured.jpg`).
- Verify license on the object page before reuse; this pack only includes **Public Domain Mark** (Wellcome) and **CC0 / Met Open Access**.
- Credit on-site: title · creator (if known) · museum · license.
- Therapeutic tone: no shock medical imagery, no stigma, no false copyright claims.
- Prefer crop + collage over unaltered full plates for the house look.

## CMS import
Each folder has `post.json` + `urls.json`. Use `slug` as the Art post slug; attach images in filename order; set featured from `featured_filename`.
