About Us

A space to understand yourself.

MindState is a student-led mental wellbeing and media platform for young people. We bring together psychology, personal stories, art, and everyday reflection to help you understand yourself and feel a little less alone.

Student life can come with pressure, uncertainty, and questions about who you are. We want caring for your mind to feel natural, accessible, and part of everyday life.

Our mission & vision

Make understanding your mind a normal part of everyday life. We imagine a world where people can express themselves and care for their wellbeing without shame or judgment.

What matters to us

Honest expression

Real voices, student stories, and room to create.

Everyday wellbeing

Reflection, learning, and small habits that support you.

Connection

A welcoming community where experiences can be shared.

What we’re building

Our website is an editorial home for articles, stories, ideas, and art. Alongside it, we’re planning a wellbeing app with mood and sleep tracking, private journaling, mindfulness, educational resources, and community.

MindState supports wellbeing, reflection, and education. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, or crisis support, and does not replace professional mental healthcare.

Built by students

MindState is a collaborative project with seven founders, bringing together people across product, design, development, editorial, marketing, and community.
