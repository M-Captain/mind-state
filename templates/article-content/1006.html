YOU WERE MY MEANING

I wanted to live loudly,

dance in the rain,

feel the sun on my skin,

let the wind run through my hair.

Then you came along.

Late-night conversations.

Laughing until we couldn’t breathe.

Those quiet moments

when a look said everything.

Somehow,

I mistook you for home.

I made you my light

until I forgot

I could shine on my own.

And when you left,

I thought I had lost everything.

But maybe

I had only lost

the version of me

that existed around you.

And maybe

finding myself again

is the meaning

I was looking for all along.
