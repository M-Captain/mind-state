1. What MindState Is

MindState is a student-led, members-only editorial community for honest conversations about mental health, culture, society and being human. We publish articles (essays, personal essays, poems, fiction, lifestyle pieces) and art posts in English and German.

These guidelines keep our readers, our contributors and the platform safe. They are friendly, but they are not optional: by contributing to MindState you agree to follow them.

Important: MindState is not therapy, diagnosis or crisis support. Nothing published on MindState replaces professional help.

3. Illustrations for the Platform

Members can draw illustrations that are used in different sections and areas of the MindState platform (for example page headers, section dividers, empty states and small spot illustrations), to make MindState playful and good looking.

  • The same rule applies: hand-drawn by people, and AI only to digitise them without drastic change.
  • Every platform illustration has its meaning explained in English or German, translated manually by the editorial team.
  • The artist is credited (by name, pen name or anonymously).
  • Platform illustrations are not posted like articles or art posts. They go on their own Illustrations contribution page (an upcoming feature). Admins review them there and choose where each one is placed.

4. How to Submit

Upcoming feature: The online submission flow and status tracker are not built yet. Until they launch, the MindState editorial team submits and tracks your piece with you. The rules apply now.

Articles

1. Complete your member profile (name or pen name, short bio; photo optional).

2. Choose the type and the language (English or German). Add a title, a short summary and your text, in your own words.

3. Add a content warning (write "None" only if truly none applies).

4. Add up to 5 images, mark exactly 1 as featured, and add the source link and licence for every image.

5. Choose to publish under your name, a pen name or anonymously, tick the consent and licence checkbox and submit.

6. Respond to admin notes if changes are requested. After approval, the editorial team arranges the manual translation.

Art posts

1. Complete your member profile.

2. Upload up to 5 images of your work. Illustrations must be hand-drawn.

3. For each image, write a description and its meaning in English or German.

4. Add a content warning, tick the consent and licence checkbox and submit. The editorial team translates your descriptions manually before publication.

You keep ownership of your work.

5. Translation and Audio

Rule: Translation of articles, art descriptions, etc. must NOT use AI, Google Translate, or similar services. It is done manually by the MindState editorial team.
  • This covers all text, in both directions: English to German and German to English.
  • Where possible, a second editor checks the translation, and the author may review it.
Exception: Online services (e.g. Google text-to-speech) may be used ONLY for voice narration, and ONLY on text that the editorial team has written or translated manually, read exactly as published WITHOUT ANY CHANGES. These services must not translate, rewrite, summarise or edit anything.

6. Working Together

  • Collaboration: artists and writers can work together. Collaborations are published as a dual contribution with both people credited. Both agree to the final version.
  • Feedback & Requests space: report bugs, suggest features, request a poem, article or art piece on a topic, or appeal a moderation decision once.
  • Admin review: the admin team reviews every submission, approves or disapproves it and adds notes on disapproval explaining what needs to change.
  • Member profiles are mandatory for everyone who posts; photos are optional. Every member gets a personal dashboard with views and stats for their own posts.
  • Until the collaboration space and the Feedback & Requests space launch, arrange collaborations through the editorial team, send feedback through the contact options on the About page, and report tech bugs as GitHub issues.

7. Safety, Content Warnings and Helplines

  • A content warning is required on every submission (e.g. self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, abuse, substance use, grief, violence).
  • Avoid graphic descriptions of methods of self-harm or suicide. Focus on experience, recovery, support and hope.
  • Be kind. Never try to identify an anonymous author. No harassment, hate speech, plagiarism, AI-generated illustrations or machine-translated text.

8. For Tech Members

  • All tech work happens through GitHub. Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA).
  • Pick or open an issue and agree the scope with the core tech team.
  • Work on a branch: feature/, fix/, docs/, chore/ (and hotfix/ for the core team only).
  • Make small, focused commits, run the linter and tests, then open a pull request with a description and screenshots for visual changes.
  • The core team reviews the code and the staging preview. At least one core tech team member must approve before merge.
  • Never commit secrets. Production must never run with DEBUG enabled.
Rule: No direct pushes to main. Every change reaches main through a pull request reviewed and approved by the core tech team.

There is one core tech team, with a maximum of 8 members, with clearly defined roles. It reviews and manages the platform at all times. Final roles and people are to be confirmed by the core team.

9. Design System in Brief

The live site at mindstate.space is the official MindState design system. Reuse its colours, fonts, radii and CSS variables; do not add new ones.

  • Google Sans Flex (weights 400, 500, 600) for body text and all UI: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Google+Sans+Flex. SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Google+Sans+Flex/license).
  • Instrument Serif for display, the MINDSTATE wordmark and italics: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif. SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif/license).
  • Colours: cream #FEF0D8 / #fff0d7, warm dark #292021, ink #060606, off-white #F6F6F6 (text on dark), art paper beige #e9ddc8, muted taupe #c7b9a5, reading text #514b43, forest green #28513b.
  • Shapes: dark pill buttons, outline pills, rounded dark panels and borderless image cards.
  • Uppercase for nav, eyebrows and footer links; titles in sentence case.
  • Tone: warm, student-led, sincere, plain words.

  • Member profiles and personal dashboards

  • Admin review dashboard
  • Feedback & Requests space
  • Collaboration space for artists and writers
  • Illustrations contribution page
  • EN/DE language toggle, reading time and audio version
  • Crisis resources bar, image licence checker and privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible or Umami)

How to pick one up

1. Pick or propose a feature through a GitHub issue. Check existing issues first.

2. Discuss it with the core tech team in the issue and agree the scope before writing code.

3. Work on a branch (e.g. feature/submission-tracker).

4. Open a pull request for core-team review. No direct pushes to main.

Writers, artists and editors are welcome to contribute too: share ideas and feedback, and test new features on their staging previews.
